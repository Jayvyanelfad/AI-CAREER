HOW LEARNING WORKS

Learning shouldn’t feel
like reading a manual.

Lessons move through a repeatable story-driven sequence: meet a situation, understand the problem, learn the concept, see an example, try a challenge, and take away a useful idea.

Course activities vary; the six-stage sequence is shown in the Introduction to AI & ML learning experience.

  1. 01StoryStart with a situation that makes the problem real.
  2. 02DiscoveryUnderstand what is happening and why it matters.
  3. 03ConceptLearn the technical idea behind it.
  4. 04ExampleSee the idea applied in practice.
  5. 05ChallengeTry the idea yourself.
  6. 06TakeawayLeave with a useful mental model or skill.

A FEW ANSWERS

Good to know.

How does the Career Assessment work?

It considers your answers across eight career-interest dimensions and presents a Career Profile with directions to explore.

What happens after I complete it?

You can review your Career Profile, explore your top directions and continue to courses connected to those interests.

How are courses connected to my Career Profile?

The catalog includes career-to-course recommendations, so you can explore learning paths related to a direction in your profile.

Can I track my learning progress?

Enrolled course progress is saved as lessons are completed and is available through your learning pages.

Are courses free?

Check the course catalog for each course’s current level and access details.
